Railway Station & Line Management System

ISEP · Group project · Sep 2025 – Jan 2026 · source →

Managing 64,000+ European railway stations meant leaning hard on graph theory — topological sort, MST, BFS, 2D-trees and AVL trees to keep lookups fast. The data layer was a standalone Oracle PL/SQL module: triggers, stored procedures, and a physics engine for train scheduling. On the hardware side, RISC-V routines and a Raspberry Pi Pico with a DHT11 sensor simulated real railway signaling.

JavaRISC-VOracle PL/SQLRaspberry Pi PicoGraph AlgorithmsJUnit

Flight Management System

ISEP · Group project · Feb 2026 – Jun 2026 · source →

Scheduling and operational logic for a flight management system — the first project where our database was actually wired into the application instead of standing alone. Used Hibernate to open the connection, then wrote a custom request layer over TCP and UDP rather than leaning on Hibernate's defaults. Supporting network infrastructure (VoIP, DHCP, OSPF, ACLs) configured and tested alongside it.
